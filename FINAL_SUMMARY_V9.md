# Version 9 — Theme + Advanced Accounting UI

- Persistent light/dark mode in Settings.
- Accent color choices for primary buttons and UI highlights.
- High-contrast palette designed to avoid light text on light backgrounds and dark text on dark backgrounds.
- Rebuild-on-apply preserves business services and database state.
- POS now supports invoice-level discount and tax rate with live recalculation.
- Sale list supports full-return workflow with stock and accounting reversal.
- Accounting page includes trial balance and recent journal entries.
- Invoice previews include subtotal, discount, tax, total, paid and remaining.

The stable v8 behavior remains the base; v9 is a separate development copy.
